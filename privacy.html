ShoreList UK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

ShoreList UK is an app for shore anglers in the UK. It is run by Troy Fitzsimmons. If you have a question about your data, or want to use any of your rights below, email troyfitz96@icloud.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, your angler handle, a password (stored scrambled, so we cannot read it), your date of birth, and optionally your home waters, country and a profile photo.
  • Your fishing log: your sessions (start and end time, method, target species, notes) and your fish (species, length, bait, rig, notes, photos, time).
  • Your marks and where you fished: the marks you save (a name and a position), and the position of each session and fish. They are stored securely, encrypted when they travel and when they are stored, and protected so that only you can read them in the app. They are saved in your account so they come back if you change phone. No other angler is ever shown them, and we do not look at them or share them. The only other use is anonymous totals, such as how many of a species were caught in a region lately, which never show who caught them or exactly where.
  • Community, if you use it: your friends and friend requests, posts, comments, reactions and tags, messages, clubs you join and the matches and chat in them, and the details you choose to share about a session.
  • Conditions: the weather, tide, wind, moon and pressure at your position, saved with your sessions and fish.
  • Games and leaderboards: the games you play, your scores, the photos you submit as proof, and your votes on other people's fish.
  • Technical information: which version of the app you use, your phone type (iPhone or Android), and when you last opened it. If the app crashes, a short error report: the error text and where in the app it happened. It never includes your photos, your position or anything you typed.

Your location

We ask for your location to show weather and tides near you, to start a session at your mark, to save marks and fish, and to end a session by itself when you go far from your mark.

  • We use it while you are using the app and while a session is running. During a session the app keeps following your position in the background, so it can end the session when you leave. On iPhone you will see the blue location bar while this happens. On Android you will see a notification. It stops when the session ends.
  • We do not track you at other times.
  • Where you fished is never shown to other anglers. Not the position, not the name of a mark, and not the notes you write. Fish and sessions you share show what was caught and the conditions, never the place.
  • You can turn location off in your phone's settings. Parts of the app will then not work.

Your photos

Photos you add to fish are stored so you can see them again, and shown to others only where you share them (for example a leaderboard or a game). Before a photo is uploaded, we remove the location and camera details hidden inside it.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: to store your log, work out your stats and best days, run games and leaderboards, and show your profile to other anglers as described here.
  • To keep it safe and working: to stop abuse, fix crashes, and see which version of the app people use.
  • We do not sell your information, and the app has no adverts and no advertising trackers.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage, in Ireland (EU).
  • Anthropic, the company behind Claude, receives a fish photo only when you tap "Identify it for me" (or ask for a leaderboard check), so it can suggest the species and, if the fish is on a match measure, read its length. We do not send your name, email or position with it.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather. Your phone asks it for the weather at your position, so it can see that position and your internet address.
  • WorldTides provides tide times. Our server asks it about a spot rounded to a square of about 5 km, with nothing about you.
  • Apple and Google run the app stores and the test programmes (TestFlight and Google Play testing) and collect their own information about you.

What other anglers can see

  • Your angler handle and profile photo.
  • The fish you log: the species, size, date and picture, to your friends only unless you choose otherwise. In Profile ("Who can see my fish") you can change that to nobody, or to everyone. Never where it was caught.
  • Posts you share, to your friends, to everyone or to a club, as you choose. Sessions you share show only the details you tick.
  • Games and leaderboards you take part in, with your handle and the fish you submit (without the place).
  • Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other anglers.

You can turn Community off in Profile. Then you have a private account: other anglers cannot find, add, message or see you, and you do not appear on leaderboards.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your log, photos, marks, posts, messages and profile are deleted. Fish you submitted to a game are removed from that game's score, and a game you hosted passes to another player. Copies may stay in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a short time until they expire.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile and tap "Delete my account". It cannot be undone.

Children

You must be 13 or over to use ShoreList UK. Clubs are for adults only, and you confirm you are 18 or over before you can use them. We ask for your date of birth to check this and to place under-16s on the junior leaderboard. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child under 13 has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and get a copy of it. You can delete your account yourself in the app, or email us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
