ShoreList UK

A fishing diary for UK shore anglers.

Log your sessions and fish, see the tide, wind, swell and weather for your mark, identify what you've caught, and keep track of your personal bests across 118 UK sea species.

Your marks stay yours. Other anglers never see where you fish.
